MUTABLE DREAMS 1.3 — PRIVATE REVIEW BUILD

NEW WORLDS
5 original Lo-Fi kits / 40 samples: Dust Room, Paper Machines, Soft Static,
Found Objects and Velour Tape. Use one kit and three synth tracks, or six
synth tracks. Dormant synth settings survive rack changes. Shape each pad's
pitch, level, soft filter, tail and grain send; record or generate drum steps.
48 additional tone presets and 16 Biomes bring the totals to 336 sounds and
108 musical Biomes plus Blank. The additions combine warm keys, rounded bass,
reeds, resonances and dust differently instead of duplicating one ensemble.

MORE MOVEMENT
Two independently routed LFOs per synth track: 12 in six-synth mode, or 6 in
kit mode, plus the existing Breathe movement. Each offers Triangle, Sine,
Bloom or Sigh and an independent 0.5–20 second cycle. Route to filter, colour,
pan, grain send, shared-effect sends, Warps blend or soft amplitude swell.
These are compact Tides-inspired controls, not a full emulation of a module.
No additional audio tape or effect buffer is allocated. Sends need a base
level, Filter needs a filter, and Warps needs a blend value. Both movements
can address the same target; audio parameters remain bounded. LFOs run while
the selected synth voice is active; they are not MIDI-clock-synced LFOs.
Settings are stored in the complete Biome. Sound slots retain their existing
format; the new routing and LFO B settings remain part of the Biome.

SEQUENCES / HARMONY
Forward, Reverse, Pendulum, Orbit and Seeded traversal, an independent offset
and gentle deterministic velocity feel. Stored steps stay intact. Live note
recording follows the remapped step. Four new generators (Petals, Tideline,
Stars, Answers) join Sparse, Arpeggio, Pulse and Drift. Generating replaces the
selected track's phrase; ENV undoes. Four longer journeys join the existing
four progressions. Choose 1–16 bars per chord and Original or Nearby root
moves. Nearby reduces root transposition jumps; it is not polyphonic voice
leading. Sequences, recording and chord progressions already existed in 1.2;
these are extensions, not claims that all sequencing is new.

READABILITY / KEEP YOUR WORK
Unsaved Biome-change protection; larger readable type, seven calm page
palettes, contextual headings, transparent text over a darker field, tiny
responsive organisms, and cached organic A/D and LFO graphs. The panel remains
240 x 240 pixels. The envelope graph describes the actual one-shot attack and
decay; MIDI gate/release is separate. A new full ADSR engine is not claimed.
Every held function key opens help; releasing a held key does not perform its
short press. Motion can be disabled on the Look page.

SCOPE
Bluetooth is excluded. Public 1.0, 1.1 and 1.2 are unchanged; Syntherella is
untouched. The existing MIDI functions remain; this is not a new BLE release.
V16 / M-UPGRADE and a new stock recovery roundtrip are not qualified here.
Keep the original backup to downgrade. New v9 patches need this 1.3 candidate;
old 1.0/1.1/1.2 and earlier private v8 Biomes migrate with neutral additions.
