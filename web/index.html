GROWING THROUGH YOUR FEEDBACK

Thank you for listening.

You asked for Biomes that sound further apart, mute states you can read at a glance, more scales and independent MIDI control. Those requests are in 1.4. Thank you for making music with this instrument and telling us where it can grow.

Every voice is yours.

Use six freely assignable synth tracks, or switch to a kit with three synth tracks. The three dormant synth settings are kept when you change modes. Choose from 352 sound presets while keeping your notes, rhythm, levels and sends. An empty Biome is a starting point for a world of your own.

Chance, with a memory.

Euclidean patterns, swing, accents and repeats meet thirteen shared scales. Lock a seed with ARP, make a variation with REC, and undo it with ENV. Freeze, reverse or transpose the granular cloud as your rhythm keeps moving.

Room to be clear.

Full-rate granular recording, gently overlapping grains and a cleaner signal path bring more detail. Open the filter for clarity or close it for warmth. Gentle resonance and fundamental-led presets keep softer sounds within easy reach. FULL and ECO offer different processing budgets. Automatic admission limits remain in place. The release qualification is documented separately. Dense combinations may require ECO.

01 / FIND YOUR SOUND

48 new Biomes

Distinct ambient, rhythmic, resonant, bass-led and Lo-Fi ensembles sit closer together in the browse order. There are now 156 musical factory Biomes plus Blank, and 352 sound presets. Your existing factory IDs and User Banks remain in place.

02 / HEAR THE HARMONY

Five more scales

Ukrainian Dorian, Harmonic Minor, Double Harmonic Minor, Harmonic Major and Double Harmonic Major bring fresh colours to sequences and chord journeys. Your notes remain editable.

03 / PLAY YOUR WAY

MIDI per track

Assign each track to its own USB MIDI channel, let several share one, or turn note input off. Channel 10 can play the Lo-Fi kit, and the channel-16 macro shortcut can be disabled when you need notes there.

On Home, a muted track dims across its whole row and meter and says MUTED. Clearer type, stronger contrast and quiet page-responsive organisms make the 240 × 240 screen easier to read. The existing unsaved-Biome guard and contextual held-key help remain close at hand. The browser edition includes the same Biome atlas and a live FM-1 editor.

Browser 1.4 saves have their own key; import a patch file to move an older browser world across. Before updating the FM-1, save a complete .mddb bank backup and export unsaved live edits separately as JSON. All changes · Illustrated guide · Measured qualification · Browsable source archive.

VERSION 1.4 / INSTALL WITH CARE

Keep what you made. Explore further.

Save your Biome, back up saved User Banks as .mddb and export unsaved live edits as a separate JSON file. Disconnect the live editor before updating. Connect only the FM-1 you intend to update; keep USB and power connected until its identity is verified.

The exact 1.4 package was installed on the FM-1, returned to original V15 from a device that had entered through V16, and reinstalled with a byte-identical User Bank restore. M-UPGRADE installed manufacturer V16 during earlier testing but rejected the custom Mutable Dreams package. Use the browser or Python installer for this firmware. No direct route from every other custom firmware is claimed.

Physical TRS MIDI awaits an external cable test. Bluetooth MIDI and arbitrary WAV import are not included. A reported black screen after an interrupted third-party installation remains under investigation; keep power connected throughout an update and consult the recovery guide if the device fails to identify.

WITH GRATITUDE / OUR ROOTS

From a generous idea,
new worlds can grow.

Mutable Dreams owes its foundations to Émilie Gillet and Mutable Instruments. Their instruments and openly shared code made this exploration possible. Selected original algorithms from Plaits, Rings and the new Elements Strike subset form part of our sound engine, with their original credits and MIT notices preserved.

Our contribution is a six-voice instrument for the FM-1: editable Biomes, generative rhythms, a custom granular bus, and an interface that invites discovery. We want to carry that spirit of curiosity into something approachable, expressive, and unmistakably its own.
