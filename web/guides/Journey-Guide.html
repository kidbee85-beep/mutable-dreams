A little path
through your worlds.

Journey connects up to eight musical sections. Let one sound world unfold into another, keeping a favourite bass line or texture alive across the change.

Open the playable workbench →

Make your first Journey

  1. Start with a Biome you enjoy. Shape its sounds and sequence while stopped.
  2. Press SEQ through Sequence Flow, then Journey. From Kit Steps, press SEQ again. The browser also has direct Journey page buttons.
  3. In Arrange, set Mode = Once and Length = 2. Section 1 can stay on This Biome, which means your complete editable home Biome.
  4. Choose section 2 and select a factory Biome. PRESET also changes this section's source, without replacing your home project.
  5. Press SEQ for Timing. Set each section's bar length and repeats. Four bars is a gentle starting point.
  6. Press SEQ for Anchors. Choose an incoming section and a track; turn Carry on to keep that sound and pattern through its entrance.
  7. Press PLAY. The display follows the playing section. Unkept voices dissolve over the final sixteenth, incoming voices fade in, and shared grain, echo and room memories continue.
  8. Press Stop to restore your editable home Biome. Use GLO → User Banks to name and save the whole Journey in one of your sixteen Biome slots. SAVE outside Banks also stores the current project.

What this private build supports

All 272 factory entries, including Blank, and your current custom home Biome can be a Journey source. Up to eight sections are stored with your User Biome and exported patch. PLAY starts at section 1. Stop restores your editable home project. Current saves use patch format v12 (2604 bytes); keep the pre-update backup for returning to public 1.5.

Willow Bow, Aero Flute, Felt Mallet, Silk Ensemble, Copper Bloom, Vowel Bloom and the new Biomes are available to Journey. Authored factory effects now match direct recall, including the hand-percussion worlds. Living Links and captured Biome Drift are also available elsewhere in this private instrument; this guide describes the section arranger.

A kit rack has three synths and one kit. Across a kit/synth rack change, only tracks 1–3 can carry. In a kit-to-kit change, carrying track 4 also carries the entire kit. A muted carried track stays muted. Shared grain, delay and room memories continue; this is a section dissolve inside one active rack, not two simultaneous complete racks.

A Journey can reference factory sources and its own saved home Biome. Referencing several separate User Bank slots is not implemented. The undo buffer stages upcoming sections during playback; Stop restores the home project.
