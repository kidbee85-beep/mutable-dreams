# Mutable Dreams 2.0 · From a Biome to a piece

Mutable Dreams is a free, open-source six-track instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, made by Kid Bunka. Start with a sound world, let its patterns evolve, then arrange the moments you want to keep.

## Find a sound and make it yours

Explore **272 factory Biomes including Blank** and **632 tone presets**. The expanded library includes felt mallets, hand percussion, rounded basses, bowed sounds and slowly breathing ensembles. Filter the Biome Browser by family and keep favorites.

## Give evolving patterns a shape

- **Journey Arranger** plays up to eight sections. Set the duration and repeats, then carry a voice and its pattern into the next section. Chapel of Fog, Pearl Fern and Copper Step come with a Journey loop. The other 269 factory Biomes open with Journey Off.
- **Sequencer Grid** gives all six tracks a shared view. Each track has its own pattern length. Record phrases from the keyboard, adjust accents and gate lengths, copy or transpose a phrase and queue a one-bar fill.
- **Living Links and Biome Drift** add two ways to move a sound. A percussion hit can move another track's pan, and Drift can blend between two captured settings. **MIDI Learn** maps an external control to a destination with pickup.

## Play and record

- **USB audio at 44.1 kHz** carries the stereo mix on inputs 1–2 and individual mono voices on inputs 3–8. The mono voices are taken before pan and shared effects. Local monitoring can be muted while the USB recording continues.
- **USB Screen Mirror** shows the device display on a computer. The browser instrument runs on its own and includes a live editor for the FM-1.
- **Live Atlas and the larger track view** make the active voices easier to follow. Hold a function key to see the controls for that screen.

Before updating, export a saved bank backup and your edited live Biome. Use the project installer and keep the official stock file known to work on your FM-1. The guide covers installation, recall and returning to stock.

**Hardware coverage.** The preceding 2.0 build was tested on the available FM-1 from Mutable Dreams 1.5 and stock V15 and V16. This release, build `4442417D`, adds the three factory arrangements. The UC25HQ80 (`B36014`) flash checks passed offline. Installation, saving, a cold start and return to stock with this exact build on a UC25HQ80 device are still awaiting confirmation.

Mutable Dreams for the **SMK-37** is a separate adaptation in development. Model-specific availability will follow hardware testing.
