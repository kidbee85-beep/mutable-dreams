MUTABLE DREAMS 1.0 — FROM THE FM-1

Eight twelve-second stereo recordings of the opening Biomes, captured from
an actual M-VAVE FM-1 over USB: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM.

These are unmastered examples at one controlled master setting. No gain
normalization, equalization or other processing was applied after capture.
Their levels vary with the arrangement and the number of sounding voices.
Recordings.json contains the measured levels and exact firmware SHA-256.

For an editable, longer audition, open Mutable-Dreams.html in the parent
folder, start the instrument and select the matching Biome.
