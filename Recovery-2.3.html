Mutable Dreams / FM-1

Update carefully.
Keep a way back.

Before changing firmware, back up your saved banks, export your edited live Biome separately and note Favorites. An update or return to stock can clear saved data. Keep USB and power connected until the installer reports completion.

Instrument and bootloader are separate.

The Mutable Dreams 2.3 package writes the instrument application. It does not automatically replace the bootloader. The optional m-bootloader by Julius Vitkauskas (MIT) is a separate recovery project. Its recovery menu can help when an application will not start; it is not protection against every possible failure.

Check the exact flash chip.

Our 2.3 tests used a Puya-chip FM-1. This exact application and m-bootloader 2.4 still need full qualification on UC25HQ80 (JEDEC 0xB36014). A logo, casing or purchase date does not identify the flash chip reliably. Do not install a bootloader on an unqualified chip based on those clues.

If the device does not answer.

A browser updater needs a working USB update connection. A black screen with no USB response can require a separate hardware recovery process; repeated browser attempts cannot establish a missing USB connection. Read the original recovery project's instructions for your exact board before proceeding.
