GENERATIVE, PLAYABLE, YOURS

Let it grow.
Then change its direction.

Play the weather

Six generative tracks. Four immediate macros: Density, Motion, Colour and Space. Shape the piece as it unfolds.

Put your hands in it

Sixteen pads for harmony, textures, space and form. Capture a Gesture and recall six Moments while you play.

Choose an instrument

Play engine tones with up to six voices, or explore Plaits, Braids, Rings and Elements in the POLY section.

Let a rule answer

Seven LOGIC rules can address the tracks, POLY or SYNTH. Eight SYNTH engines bring a different kind of voice into the garden.

Give it a room

Warps and Rings processing, Clouds / Beads, echo, hall and granular layers. Start gently and listen to the space between notes.

Keep your own workflow

Host transport, Q-Links, project state and a stereo master plus six dry stereo track pairs where the host supports them.

DEEP / CLASSIC

More space.
The earlier character stays available.

Deep brings fuller processing, stereo hall, ensemble and FM / VA unison. Classic keeps the earlier sound and older saved-project behaviour. Voice limits and load protection depend on the chosen instruments; Mac tests are not MPC performance measurements.

0.9.13 is a preview for Akai Gen 2 MPC models. Kid Bunka has tested and approved it on his Gen 2 MPC. This does not certify every model / OS combination. Older MPC compatibility is being investigated and is not confirmed.

Use only a package confirmed for your exact model and MPC OS. This is an external plugin, not an MPC firmware replacement. One active instance is currently supported.
