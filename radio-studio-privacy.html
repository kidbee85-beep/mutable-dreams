Radio Studio privacy

Mutable Dreams Radio Studio is a desktop application used by Kid Bunka to produce and broadcast original generative music. Its Google connection operates livestreams on the connected YouTube channel.

Updated 15 November 2026. This notice describes the desktop connection, rather than the separate policies of YouTube, Radiocast or GitHub.

YouTube access

With your explicit Google authorization, the app reads the connected channel identity, existing livestream settings and broadcast status. It creates and binds its own broadcasts, starts them and ends them when requested. Google groups these operations under a broader YouTube permission. Radio Studio does not use that permission to delete videos or manage comments, ratings or subscriptions.

Storage on your Mac

The Google desktop-client configuration and refresh token are stored in the macOS Keychain. Access tokens are held in memory. Non-secret broadcast identifiers, an ownership marker and pending-operation state are stored locally so retries can reuse the same broadcast. The existing OBS stream key is read locally and is not replaced. Secrets are not included in ordinary diagnostic output.

Where data goes

API requests containing channel and broadcast information go to Google over HTTPS. When you start YouTube output, OBS sends audio and video to YouTube. When you enable the separate RC-192 radio output, radio audio goes to Radiocast. Radio Studio does not sell Google user data, send it to advertising services or use it to train AI models.

The app follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

YouTube and RC-192 can be started and stopped separately. Revoke the Google connection by removing Mutable Dreams Radio Studio from your Google account connections. Remove its saved credential in macOS Keychain Access by searching for com.kidbunka.radio.youtube. Local broadcast state is under Library/Application Support/Mutable Dreams Radio Studio/YouTube in your Mac user folder and can be removed after stopping the app.
