Every voice is yours.

Fill all six tracks with percussion, resonant strings, soft chords, or any combination. Choose from 128 sound presets while keeping your notes, rhythm, levels and sends. An empty Biome is a starting point for a world of your own.

Chance, with a memory.

Euclidean patterns, swing, accents and repeats meet eight shared scales. Lock a seed with ARP, make a variation with REC, and undo it with ENV. Freeze, reverse or transpose the granular cloud as your rhythm keeps moving.

Room to be clear.

Full-rate granular recording, gently overlapping grains and a cleaner signal path bring more detail. Open the filter for clarity or close it for warmth. Gentle resonance and fundamental-led presets keep softer sounds within easy reach. FULL offers richer processing; dense combinations automatically use ECO to protect playback on the FM-1.

WITH GRATITUDE / OUR ROOTS

From a generous idea,
new worlds can grow.

Mutable Dreams owes its foundations to Émilie Gillet and Mutable Instruments. Their instruments and openly shared code made this exploration possible. Selected original algorithms from Plaits and the modal resonator from Rings form part of our sound engine, with their original credits and MIT notices preserved.

Our contribution is a six-voice instrument for the FM-1: editable Biomes, generative rhythms, a custom granular bus, and an interface that invites discovery. We want to carry that spirit of curiosity into something approachable, expressive, and unmistakably its own.
