A finer touch.

Two new performance options in the private2.0 workbench. Your usual MIDI channel and CC mappings remain available.

Fine macro controllers

With CH16 Macros On in the MIDI page, channel16 CC16–19 still control Density, Motion, Colour and Space. Ordinary seven-bit controllers keep their exact original behaviour.

A controller with high-resolution output may also send CC48–51 as the matching fine values. The instrument combines the two messages before updating its existing smoothed, 0–1000 musical controls. USB, TRS and BLE keep separate controller caches.

Choose a pitch-wheel range

Use standard RPN0,0 on the track's MIDI channel:

  1. CC101=0, CC100=0: select pitch-bend sensitivity.
  2. CC6=12, CC38=0: an octave up or down. Add cents with CC38, for example50 for half a semitone.
  3. CC101=127, CC100=127: finish editing the parameter.

The range is limited to24 semitones; the original default is2. A new track MIDI route or reboot returns the default. These are temporary controller-session settings, not a new saved Biome parameter. Reset Controllers centres the wheel; it keeps the negotiated range. Selecting an NRPN cancels RPN selection so unrelated data cannot alter the wheel range.
